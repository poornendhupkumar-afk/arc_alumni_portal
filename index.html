About ARC

The Alumni Relations Cell

ARC is the bridge between our institution's past, present, and future. We nurture lifelong bonds between graduates and the community that shaped them.

  • Facilitate meaningful mentorship between senior alumni and current students across all disciplines.
  • Organize networking events, reunions, and industry talks that bridge academia and the professional world.
  • Maintain a vibrant, searchable directory of graduates to enable collaboration and hiring within the community.
  • Celebrate alumni achievements and amplify success stories that inspire generations to come.
ARC Alumni
